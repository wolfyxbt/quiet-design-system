Keeping a small site small

Every page on this site is one column of text. There are no cards, no borders, and no background fills. Hierarchy comes from four shades of gray and three font weights.

The whole thing is driven by a single file, tokens.css, and a handful of classes. If you want the details, the source is public.

What I left out

  • Accent colors, except for one four-pixel tick.
  • Hover backgrounds. Rows underline their title and nothing else.
  • Anything that needs a shadow to be understood.
A layout this plain only works if the type is set carefully, so that is where the effort goes.

That's the entire system. It fits in your head, which is the point.
